## 💡 The Problem

College students often experience academic pressure, social challenges, and emotional distress. Although some students turn to AI chatbots to express their feelings, they may not fully understand the privacy implications of sharing sensitive personal information with third-party platforms.

There is a need for a trusted, college-based platform that encourages students to seek support without compromising their sense of privacy.

## ✨ Our Solution

HAVEN proposes a privacy-conscious mental wellness ecosystem within the college community, connecting students with appropriate support while maintaining clear boundaries around sensitive information.

### 🚀 Key Features

- **🔐 Privacy-Centred Access:** Offers anonymous access and official college email login options to accommodate different student preferences.
- **😊 Daily Mood Tracking:** Enables students to record their emotional state through a quick, simple check-in.
- **🤖 AI-Assisted Support:** Provides an initial layer of support for mild distress, with appropriate safeguards and clear limitations.
- **🧑‍⚕️ Counselor Connection:** Facilitates access to institutional counselors when additional support is needed.
- **🚨 Risk-Based Escalation:** Proposes a structured escalation process for high-risk situations, subject to appropriate consent, safeguarding protocols, and institutional policies.
- **🏫 College-Centred Ecosystem:** Focuses on support within an individual college community rather than combining students' sensitive information across unrelated institutions.

## 🌟 What Makes HAVEN Different?

HAVEN puts **student privacy, institutional accountability, and timely support** at the centre of its design.

Rather than treating mental wellness as just another chatbot interaction, HAVEN proposes a college-governed approach that combines everyday emotional check-ins, AI-assisted support, and pathways to human assistance.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To create a safer and more trustworthy environment where college students can acknowledge their emotions, seek help without unnecessary barriers, and access appropriate mental wellness support.
